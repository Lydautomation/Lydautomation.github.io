How I Approach Automation

I approach automation by first understanding the problem and the process behind it, then designing automation that reduces unnecessary manual work, connects information effectively, and keeps people involved where human judgment is needed.

01 / Identify The Problem

Start by identifying the problem, where the current process is creating difficulty, and what needs to improve.

02 / Understand The Process

Understand how the current process works, where repetitive or time-consuming tasks occur, and how information moves from one step to another.

03 / Design The Right Automation

Determine what should be automated, how the necessary tools should work together, and where human involvement should remain.

04 / Build With Human Oversight

Include the right review, approval, escalation, and handover points so people remain in control of decisions that require human judgment.
